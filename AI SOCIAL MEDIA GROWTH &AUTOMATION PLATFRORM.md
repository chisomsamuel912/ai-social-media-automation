Product Requirements Document (PRD)  
   
AI Social Media Growth & Automation Platform  
   
Status: MVP Product Definition  
Product Type: AI-powered social media management and automation platform  
Primary Users: Small business owners, creators, and service businesses  
Core Promise: Give the AI direction once. Let it handle the content work.  
   
1\. Product Vision  
   
The product is an AI-powered social media assistant that helps businesses consistently create, manage, publish, and improve their social-media content without requiring the business owner to become a social-media expert.  
   
Instead of asking users to repeatedly:  
          	•        	Think of content ideas  
          	•        	Write captions  
          	•        	Create images  
          	•        	Make videos  
          	•        	Adapt content for every platform  
          	•        	Decide when to post  
          	•        	Remember to publish  
          	•        	Monitor performance  
          	•        	Reply to customers  
          	•        	Follow up with potential customers  
   
…the AI handles most of the work.  
   
The user provides the business information and preferences once, reviews important outputs, and lets the AI operate continuously.  
   
Product philosophy  
   
          	Powerful behind the scenes. Extremely simple for the business owner.  
   
2\. Problem  
   
Small businesses often struggle with social media because they:  
          	•        	Don’t know what to post consistently.  
          	•        	Spend too much time creating content.  
          	•        	Repeat the same ideas.  
          	•        	Don’t know how to adapt content to different platforms.  
          	•        	Forget to post.  
          	•        	Don’t know what type of content performs well.  
          	•        	Struggle to create good images and videos.  
          	•        	Have difficulty responding to customers consistently.  
          	•        	Lose potential customers who show interest but don’t receive follow-up.  
          	•        	Don’t have the time or money to hire a social-media manager.  
   
Existing AI tools can generate captions, but the business owner still has to act as the content strategist, designer, scheduler, publisher, and analyst.  
   
This product aims to reduce that workload.  
   
3\. Solution  
   
The platform acts as an AI social-media strategist and assistant.  
   
The business owner creates a Content Profile once.  
   
The AI then uses that information to:  
          	1\.      	Understand the business.  
          	2\.      	Understand its audience.  
          	3\.      	Plan content.  
          	4\.      	Generate content ideas.  
          	5\.      	Write platform-specific content.  
          	6\.      	Choose suitable formats.  
          	7\.      	Generate matching images/videos/graphics.  
          	8\.      	Use the business’s own media when appropriate.  
          	9\.      	Review previous content to avoid repetition.  
          	10\.    	Identify relevant trends.  
          	11\.    	Include promotional content naturally.  
          	12\.    	Present content for approval.  
          	13\.    	Schedule and/or publish content.  
          	14\.    	Monitor performance.  
          	15\.    	Learn what works.  
          	16\.    	Improve future content.  
          	17\.    	Respond to suitable customer comments.  
          	18\.    	Identify potential customers.  
          	19\.    	Help move leads toward the business’s chosen sales channel.  
          	20\.    	Follow up with interested customers within safeguards.  
   
4\. Target Users  
   
Primary  
   
Small businesses that depend on social media for:  
          	•        	Awareness  
          	•        	Engagement  
          	•        	Leads  
          	•        	Sales  
          	•        	Customer communication  
   
Examples:  
          	•        	Fashion businesses  
          	•        	Restaurants  
          	•        	Beauty businesses  
          	•        	Coaches  
          	•        	Consultants  
          	•        	Online stores  
          	•        	Service providers  
          	•        	Personal brands  
          	•        	Local businesses  
          	•        	Education businesses  
          	•        	Finance creators  
          	•        	Real-estate businesses  
   
The product should be broad enough to support different niches while allowing the AI to behave specifically according to each business’s Content Profile.  
   
5\. Core User Experience  
   
The entire product should revolve around a very simple workflow:  
   
Setup once  
   
Business Profile → Content Profile → Brand → Platforms → Sales Channel  
   
Then repeatedly  
   
AI plans → Creates → Creates visuals → User reviews → Approves → Schedules/Publishes → AI monitors → AI learns  
   
The user should not have to understand:  
          	•        	Content pillars  
          	•        	Marketing funnels  
          	•        	Content angles  
          	•        	Platform algorithms  
          	•        	Posting strategies  
          	•        	Engagement strategies  
   
The AI handles those decisions behind the scenes.  
   
6\. Onboarding / Content Profile  
   
During initial setup, the user provides information such as:  
   
Business information  
          	•        	Business name  
          	•        	Business description  
          	•        	Products/services  
          	•        	Important business information  
   
Audience  
          	•        	Target audience  
          	•        	Location, where relevant  
          	•        	Age group, where relevant  
          	•        	Customer characteristics  
   
Content preferences  
          	•        	Tone of voice  
          	•        	Main topics  
          	•        	Topics to avoid  
          	•        	Preferred content goals  
   
Platforms  
   
The user chooses which platforms they use.  
   
Posting frequency  
   
Default:  
   
          	Let AI decide  
   
Optional:  
          	•        	3 posts/week  
          	•        	5 posts/week  
          	•        	7 posts/week  
   
Publishing mode  
          	•        	⭐ Auto Publish  
          	•        	🔔 Remind Me  
   
Sales channel  
   
The business can select one or multiple:  
          	•        	WhatsApp  
          	•        	Website/order page  
          	•        	Instagram DM  
          	•        	Facebook Messenger  
          	•        	Other supported sales channels  
   
Business-specific dates  
   
Optional:  
          	•        	Business anniversary  
          	•        	Product launch  
          	•        	Special events  
          	•        	Other important dates  
   
The AI should know common holidays and relevant events automatically.  
   
7\. AI Content Planning  
   
The AI has an Auto Pilot mode.  
   
The default experience should be:  
   
          	“You handle my content.”  
   
The AI decides:  
          	•        	What to post  
          	•        	When to post  
          	•        	Which topic to use  
          	•        	Which angle to use  
          	•        	Which format to use  
          	•        	Which platform version to create  
          	•        	Whether the post should be educational, promotional, entertaining, etc.  
   
Guide Me  
   
Users can optionally give additional instructions.  
   
Examples:  
   
          	“Focus more on saving this week.”  
   
          	“Promote my new course.”  
   
          	“Create five posts.”  
   
The AI should combine these instructions with the Content Profile.  
   
8\. Create Content Mode  
   
The second major workflow is:  
   
＋ Create Content  
   
The user provides something specific.  
   
Examples:  
   
          	“I have a new product.”  
   
          	“I want to announce our discount.”  
   
          	“Create content about budgeting.”  
   
          	“Promote this event.”  
   
The AI transforms that input into appropriate social-media content.  
   
9\. Content Variety & Memory  
   
A major product requirement is avoiding repetitive content.  
   
The AI should maintain content memory/history containing things such as:  
          	•        	Previously used topics  
          	•        	Angles  
          	•        	Hooks  
          	•        	Formats  
          	•        	Platforms  
          	•        	Promotions  
          	•        	Recent posts  
          	•        	Rejected content  
          	•        	Previously generated content  
   
Content strategy  
   
The AI should create mostly new topics, while occasionally revisiting important topics from genuinely different angles.  
   
For example:  
   
Topic: Saving money  
   
Instead of repeatedly creating:  
   
          	“5 ways to save money.”  
   
It might later create:  
          	•        	A story about someone who couldn’t save.  
          	•        	A common savings mistake.  
          	•        	A myth about saving.  
          	•        	A challenge.  
          	•        	A question.  
          	•        	A short video.  
          	•        	A practical checklist.  
   
The same broad subject can return, but the content should not feel like a duplicate.  
   
10\. Content Format Rotation  
   
The AI automatically chooses and rotates formats.  
   
Possible formats include:  
          	•        	Educational posts  
          	•        	Tips  
          	•        	Lists  
          	•        	Stories  
          	•        	Questions  
          	•        	Polls  
          	•        	Myths vs facts  
          	•        	Did-you-know  
          	•        	Relatable/humorous content  
          	•        	Short videos  
          	•        	Carousels  
          	•        	Product promotions  
          	•        	Testimonials/social proof  
          	•        	Announcements  
          	•        	Lead-generation content  
   
The user should not need to manually manage format rotation.  
   
Advanced controls can exist later.  
   
11\. Promotional Content  
   
If a business has products/services/offers, the AI should naturally include promotional content.  
   
It should not repeatedly say “Buy now.”  
   
Instead, it can rotate:  
   
Value → Engagement → Story → Promotion → Value → Promotion  
   
Promotional content should have different angles.  
   
The AI must never invent:  
          	•        	Prices  
          	•        	Discounts  
          	•        	Product features  
          	•        	Stock availability  
          	•        	Testimonials  
          	•        	Guarantees  
          	•        	Delivery information  
   
unless that information has been supplied or reliably confirmed by the business.  
   
12\. Events & Trends  
   
The AI should understand relevant:  
          	•        	Holidays  
          	•        	Seasonal events  
          	•        	Major occasions  
          	•        	Social-media trends  
   
However:  
   
          	A trend should only be used when it naturally fits the business.  
   
The AI should not force irrelevant trends into a content plan.  
   
For example, a serious financial business should not automatically participate in every viral entertainment trend.  
   
13\. Platform-Native Content  
   
One content idea should become different versions for different platforms.  
   
It should not simply copy and paste the same content everywhere.  
   
Examples:  
   
Instagram  
   
Strong hook \+ caption \+ suitable hashtags \+ visual.  
   
Facebook  
   
More conversational and potentially longer.  
   
YouTube  
   
Title \+ description \+ script/video content where appropriate.  
   
TikTok  
   
Short-form hook \+ concise video concept/script.  
   
LinkedIn  
   
More professional/business-oriented presentation.  
   
The AI should adapt content according to each platform’s natural style.  
   
14\. AI Visual Creation  
   
This is a core feature.  
   
The app should create visuals that actually fit the content.  
   
Possible outputs:  
          	•        	Images  
          	•        	Graphics  
          	•        	Carousels  
          	•        	Short videos  
          	•        	Video scripts  
          	•        	Infographics  
          	•        	Promotional visuals  
   
The visual should be based on the actual content, not generic decoration.  
   
Example:  
   
          	“3 mistakes young Nigerians make when saving money”  
   
The AI might generate a suitable infographic or short video rather than simply attaching a random finance image.  
   
15\. Business Media Library  
   
Users can upload their own:  
          	•        	Product photos  
          	•        	Videos  
          	•        	Logos  
          	•        	Brand assets  
          	•        	Other media  
   
The AI intelligently decides when to use the user’s real media.  
   
Example:  
   
A shoe business uploads photos of its shoes.  
   
For:  
   
          	“Our new black sneakers are available.”  
   
The AI should prioritize the actual product photo.  
   
For:  
   
          	“3 ways to take care of leather shoes.”  
   
The AI might generate an educational graphic/video.  
   
16\. Brand Memory  
   
The AI should remember the business’s brand identity.  
   
Possible brand information:  
          	•        	Business name  
          	•        	Logo  
          	•        	Brand colors  
          	•        	Visual style  
          	•        	Writing style  
          	•        	Tone  
          	•        	Preferred imagery  
          	•        	Video style  
          	•        	Things to avoid  
   
The AI can learn from the user’s corrections and preferences.  
   
The user can still manually adjust the brand.  
   
The goal is:  
   
          	The business should look and sound like the same business across every piece of content.  
   
17\. Content Review  
   
Before publishing, the user gets a simple Content Review screen.  
   
Example:  
   
          	Your content is ready  
   
          	7 posts created  
3 platforms  
0 recent duplicates  
   
Each post shows:  
          	•        	Platform  
          	•        	Content preview  
          	•        	Image/video  
          	•        	Caption  
          	•        	Suggested publishing time  
   
Actions:  
          	•        	🔄 Redo  
          	•        	✨ AI Edit  
          	•        	✏️ Manual Edit  
          	•        	❌ Reject  
   
Visual controls  
   
The user can also change the AI’s selected format:  
   
          	AI chose: Short Video  
   
Change format:  
          	•        	Image  
          	•        	Video  
          	•        	Carousel  
          	•        	Other available format  
   
The AI regenerates the content appropriately.  
   
18\. Missing Information  
   
The AI should not constantly interrupt the user.  
   
If information is missing but isn’t essential, it should continue.  
   
If information is important, it should clearly flag it.  
   
Example:  
   
          	Missing important information  
   
          	You mentioned a new package, but we don’t know the price or what’s included.  
   
          	Add details | Create without them  
   
The AI must never fill missing business facts with guesses.  
   
19\. Approval & Publishing  
   
User can:  
   
          	Approve All  
   
After approval:  
   
Default  
   
AI automatically schedules the content.  
   
The user can change any scheduled time later.  
   
Publishing modes  
   
Auto Publish  
   
The system publishes automatically.  
   
Remind Me  
   
The system reminds the owner when it’s time to publish, and the owner manually publishes.  
   
This keeps automation high while preserving user control.  
   
20\. Scheduling  
   
The AI determines suitable publishing times based on:  
          	•        	Platform  
          	•        	Audience  
          	•        	Content type  
          	•        	Historical performance  
          	•        	Previous results  
   
The user can change scheduled times.  
   
The system should avoid unnecessarily complicated calendar management.  
   
21\. Performance Intelligence  
   
After publishing, the app monitors performance.  
   
It should show useful information such as:  
          	•        	Engagement  
          	•        	Views  
          	•        	Reach  
          	•        	Clicks  
          	•        	Leads  
          	•        	Other relevant platform metrics  
   
But the more important function is what happens behind the scenes.  
   
The AI learns:  
   
          	“Educational videos perform better for this business.”  
   
          	“This topic consistently gets more engagement.”  
   
          	“This type of hook performs better.”  
   
It then uses those insights to improve future content.  
   
Important distinction  
   
The app shouldn’t simply become an analytics dashboard.  
   
Its purpose is:  
   
          	Analytics → Learning → Better future content  
   
22\. Trend Intelligence  
   
The AI continuously looks for relevant trends.  
   
For each potential trend, it considers:  
          	•        	Does it fit the niche?  
          	•        	Does it fit the audience?  
          	•        	Does it fit the brand?  
          	•        	Can it naturally become useful content?  
   
If yes, it can incorporate it into future content.  
   
If not, it ignores it.  
   
23\. Comment Management  
   
The AI monitors comments on published content.  
   
Simple comments  
   
The AI can automatically respond.  
   
Example:  
   
          	“Thanks\!”  
   
Business questions  
   
The AI can respond using verified business information.  
   
Uncertain/important situations  
   
The AI sends the conversation to the owner.  
   
The AI must not invent:  
          	•        	Prices  
          	•        	Stock  
          	•        	Delivery times  
          	•        	Policies  
          	•        	Product information  
          	•        	Guarantees  
   
24\. Lead Detection  
   
The AI identifies potential buying intent.  
   
Example:  
   
          	“How much is this?”  
   
          	“I want two.”  
   
          	“How can I order?”  
   
The system identifies these as potential leads.  
   
The owner receives something like:  
   
          	🔥 Potential customer  
   
          	Customer is interested in the black sneakers.  
   
25\. Lead Conversion  
   
The AI can help move interested customers toward the business’s chosen sales channel.  
   
Possible destinations:  
          	•        	WhatsApp  
          	•        	Website/order page  
          	•        	Instagram DM  
          	•        	Facebook Messenger  
   
The business chooses its preferred channel.  
   
The AI uses that information when responding to customers.  
   
26\. Customer Follow-Up  
   
The AI can follow up with interested customers.  
   
Example:  
   
          	Customer: “I’ll order later.”  
   
The system can follow up after an appropriate period.  
   
Safeguards  
          	•        	No spam.  
          	•        	Reasonable follow-up frequency.  
          	•        	Business can disable automated follow-up.  
          	•        	Customer can end communication.  
          	•        	AI does not repeatedly contact uninterested people.  
          	•        	Sensitive/uncertain conversations are escalated to the owner.  
   
27\. Customer Memory  
   
The app should remember basic useful customer context, not everything.  
   
Example:  
   
          	Sarah  
Interested in: Black sneakers  
Asked about: Size 40  
Status: Didn’t purchase  
   
If Sarah returns later, the AI can use that context to make the conversation more useful.  
   
The system should avoid collecting unnecessary personal information.  
   
28\. Dashboard  
   
The dashboard should be extremely simple.  
   
Possible main sections:  
   
🏠 Home  
   
“What needs your attention?”  
   
🤖 Auto Pilot  
   
AI’s current content plan.  
   
✨ Create Content  
   
Create something specific.  
   
📅 Schedule  
   
Upcoming content.  
   
💬 Customers  
   
Comments, leads and conversations requiring attention.  
   
📊 Results  
   
Performance and AI insights.  
   
🎨 Brand  
   
Brand identity and media.  
   
The dashboard should prioritize actions, not technical information.  
   
29\. Core Product Loop  
   
The product’s most important loop is:  
   
Business Profile  
   
↓  
   
AI understands business  
   
↓  
   
AI plans content  
   
↓  
   
AI creates posts

AI creates matching visuals  
   
↓  
   
AI adapts content for platforms  
   
↓

↓

↓  
   
AI follows up when appropriate  
   
↓  
   
Repeat  
   
This is the heart of the product.

For the first serious version, the MVP should focus on the core promise rather than trying to build everything imaginable.  
   
Must-have

          	•        	Comment management

          	•        	Basic customer memory

Later / Post-MVP  
          	•        	Payments/subscriptions  
          	•        	Advanced team collaboration  
          	•        	Multiple businesses under one account  
          	•        	Advanced analytics  
          	•        	Advanced CRM  
          	•        	More sophisticated sales automation  
          	•        	Advanced competitor analysis  
          	•        	More advanced campaign management  
          	•        	Additional social platforms  
          	•        	Enterprise features  
   
31\. Product Differentiation  
   
The product should not be positioned as:  
   
          	“An AI that writes social-media captions.”  
   
That’s too easy to compare with generic AI tools.  
   
The stronger positioning is:  
   
          	An AI social-media employee for your business.  
   
It remembers your business, plans your content, creates the posts and visuals, adapts them to each platform, publishes them, watches the results, learns what works, handles simple customer interactions, identifies leads, and helps follow up.  
   
The business owner mainly needs to:  
   
          	Set the direction → Review → Approve.  
   
32\. Key Product Principle  
   
Every feature should pass this test:  
   
          	Does this reduce work for the business owner?  
   
If a feature requires the user to constantly make decisions, configure complicated settings, or manually manage content strategy, it should either be automated or hidden under optional advanced controls.  
   
33\. Product Success  
   
The product should ultimately make a business owner feel:  
   
          	“I don’t have to worry about what I’m posting every day anymore.”  
   
The strongest measure of success isn’t simply how many posts the AI generates.  
   
It is whether the app successfully moves the user from:  
   
“I need to post something.”  
   
to:  
   
“My social media is being handled.”

          	•        	Marketing funnels  
          	•        	Content angles  
          	•        	Platform algorithms  
          	•        	Posting strategies  
          	•        	Engagement strategies  
   
The AI handles those decisions behind the scenes.  
   
6\. Onboarding / Content Profile  
   
During initial setup, the user provides information such as:  
   
Business information  
          	•        	Business name  
          	•        	Business description  
          	•        	Products/services  
          	•        	Important business information  
   
Audience  
          	•        	Target audience  
          	•        	Location, where relevant  
          	•        	Age group, where relevant  
          	•        	Customer characteristics  
   
Content preferences  
          	•        	Tone of voice  
          	•        	Main topics  
          	•        	Topics to avoid  
          	•        	Preferred content goals  
   
Platforms  
   
The user chooses which platforms they use.  
   
Posting frequency  
   
Default:  
   
          	Let AI decide  
   
Optional:  
          	•        	3 posts/week  
          	•        	5 posts/week  
          	•        	7 posts/week  
   
Publishing mode  
          	•        	⭐ Auto Publish  
          	•        	🔔 Remind Me  
   
Sales channel  
   
The business can select one or multiple:  
          	•        	WhatsApp  
          	•        	Website/order page  
          	•        	Instagram DM  
          	•        	Facebook Messenger  
          	•        	Other supported sales channels  
   
Business-specific dates  
   
Optional:  
          	•        	Business anniversary  
          	•        	Product launch  
          	•        	Special events  
          	•        	Other important dates  
   
The AI should know common holidays and relevant events automatically.  
   
7\. AI Content Planning  
   
The AI has an Auto Pilot mode.  
   
The default experience should be:  
   
          	“You handle my content.”  
   
The AI decides:  
          	•        	What to post  
          	•        	When to post  
          	•        	Which topic to use  
          	•        	Which angle to use  
          	•        	Which format to use  
          	•        	Which platform version to create  
          	•        	Whether the post should be educational, promotional, entertaining, etc.  
   
Guide Me  
   
Users can optionally give additional instructions.  
   
Examples:  
   
          	“Focus more on saving this week.”  
   
          	“Promote my new course.”  
   
          	“Create five posts.”  
   
The AI should combine these instructions with the Content Profile.  
   
8\. Create Content Mode  
   
The second major workflow is:  
   
＋ Create Content  
   
The user provides something specific.  
   
Examples:  
   
          	“I have a new product.”  
   
          	“I want to announce our discount.”  
   
          	“Create content about budgeting.”  
   
          	“Promote this event.”  
   
The AI transforms that input into appropriate social-media content.  
   
9\. Content Variety & Memory  
   
A major product requirement is avoiding repetitive content.  
   
The AI should maintain content memory/history containing things such as:  
          	•        	Previously used topics  
          	•        	Angles  
          	•        	Hooks  
          	•        	Formats  
          	•        	Platforms  
          	•        	Promotions  
          	•        	Recent posts  
          	•        	Rejected content  
          	•        	Previously generated content  
   
Content strategy  
   
The AI should create mostly new topics, while occasionally revisiting important topics from genuinely different angles.  
   
For example:  
   
Topic: Saving money  
   
Instead of repeatedly creating:  
   
          	“5 ways to save money.”  
   
It might later create:  
          	•        	A story about someone who couldn’t save.  
          	•        	A common savings mistake.  
          	•        	A myth about saving.  
          	•        	A challenge.  
          	•        	A question.  
          	•        	A short video.  
          	•        	A practical checklist.  
   
The same broad subject can return, but the content should not feel like a duplicate.  
   
10\. Content Format Rotation  
   
The AI automatically chooses and rotates formats.  
   
Possible formats include:  
          	•        	Educational posts  
          	•        	Tips  
          	•        	Lists  
          	•        	Stories  
          	•        	Questions  
          	•        	Polls  
          	•        	Myths vs facts  
          	•        	Did-you-know  
          	•        	Relatable/humorous content  
          	•        	Short videos  
          	•        	Carousels  
          	•        	Product promotions  
          	•        	Testimonials/social proof  
          	•        	Announcements  
          	•        	Lead-generation content  
   
The user should not need to manually manage format rotation.  
   
Advanced controls can exist later.  
   
11\. Promotional Content  
   
If a business has products/services/offers, the AI should naturally include promotional content.  
   
It should not repeatedly say “Buy now.”  
   
Instead, it can rotate:  
   
Value → Engagement → Story → Promotion → Value → Promotion  
   
Promotional content should have different angles.  
   
The AI must never invent:  
          	•        	Prices  
          	•        	Discounts  
          	•        	Product features  
          	•        	Stock availability  
          	•        	Testimonials  
          	•        	Guarantees  
          	•        	Delivery information  
   
unless that information has been supplied or reliably confirmed by the business.  
   
12\. Events & Trends  
   
The AI should understand relevant:  
          	•        	Holidays  
          	•        	Seasonal events  
          	•        	Major occasions  
          	•        	Social-media trends  
   
However:  
   
          	A trend should only be used when it naturally fits the business.  
   
The AI should not force irrelevant trends into a content plan.  
   
For example, a serious financial business should not automatically participate in every viral entertainment trend.  
   
13\. Platform-Native Content  
   
One content idea should become different versions for different platforms.  
   
It should not simply copy and paste the same content everywhere.  
   
Examples:  
   
Instagram  
   
Strong hook \+ caption \+ suitable hashtags \+ visual.  
   
Facebook  
   
More conversational and potentially longer.  
   
YouTube  
   
Title \+ description \+ script/video content where appropriate.  
   
TikTok  
   
Short-form hook \+ concise video concept/script.  
   
LinkedIn  
   
More professional/business-oriented presentation.  
   
The AI should adapt content according to each platform’s natural style.  
   
14\. AI Visual Creation  
   
This is a core feature.  
   
The app should create visuals that actually fit the content.  
   
Possible outputs:  
          	•        	Images  
          	•        	Graphics  
          	•        	Carousels  
          	•        	Short videos  
          	•        	Video scripts  
          	•        	Infographics  
          	•        	Promotional visuals  
   
The visual should be based on the actual content, not generic decoration.  
   
Example:  
   
          	“3 mistakes young Nigerians make when saving money”  
   
The AI might generate a suitable infographic or short video rather than simply attaching a random finance image.  
   
15\. Business Media Library  
   
Users can upload their own:  
          	•        	Product photos  
          	•        	Videos  
          	•        	Logos  
          	•        	Brand assets  
          	•        	Other media  
   
The AI intelligently decides when to use the user’s real media.  
   
Example:  
   
A shoe business uploads photos of its shoes.  
   
For:  
   
          	“Our new black sneakers are available.”  
   
The AI should prioritize the actual product photo.  
   
For:  
   
          	“3 ways to take care of leather shoes.”  
   
The AI might generate an educational graphic/video.  
   
16\. Brand Memory  
   
The AI should remember the business’s brand identity.  
   
Possible brand information:  
          	•        	Business name  
          	•        	Logo  
          	•        	Brand colors  
          	•        	Visual style  
          	•        	Writing style  
          	•        	Tone  
          	•        	Preferred imagery  
          	•        	Video style  
          	•        	Things to avoid  
   
The AI can learn from the user’s corrections and preferences.  
   
The user can still manually adjust the brand.  
   
The goal is:  
   
          	The business should look and sound like the same business across every piece of content.

Change format:

          	•        	Other available format

18\. Missing Information  
   
The AI should not constantly interrupt the user.  
   
If information is missing but isn’t essential, it should continue.  
   
If information is important, it should clearly flag it.  
   
Example:  
   
          	Missing important information  
   
          	You mentioned a new package, but we don’t know the price or what’s included.  
   
          	Add details | Create without them  
   
The AI must never fill missing business facts with guesses.  
   
19\. Approval & Publishing  
   
User can:  
   
          	Approve All  
   
After approval:  
   
Default  
   
AI automatically schedules the content.  
   
The user can change any scheduled time later.  
   
Publishing modes  
   
Auto Publish  
   
The system publishes automatically.  
   
Remind Me  
   
The system reminds the owner when it’s time to publish, and the owner manually publishes.  
   
This keeps automation high while preserving user control.  
   
20\. Scheduling  
   
The AI determines suitable publishing times based on:  
          	•        	Platform  
          	•        	Audience  
          	•        	Content type  
          	•        	Historical performance  
          	•        	Previous results  
   
The user can change scheduled times.  
   
The system should avoid unnecessarily complicated calendar management.  
   
21\. Performance Intelligence  
   
After publishing, the app monitors performance.  
   
It should show useful information such as:  
          	•        	Engagement  
          	•        	Views  
          	•        	Reach  
          	•        	Clicks  
          	•        	Leads  
          	•        	Other relevant platform metrics  
   
But the more important function is what happens behind the scenes.  
   
The AI learns:  
   
          	“Educational videos perform better for this business.”  
   
          	“This topic consistently gets more engagement.”  
   
          	“This type of hook performs better.”  
   
It then uses those insights to improve future content.  
   
Important distinction  
   
The app shouldn’t simply become an analytics dashboard.  
   
Its purpose is:  
   
          	Analytics → Learning → Better future content  
   
22\. Trend Intelligence  
   
The AI continuously looks for relevant trends.  
   
For each potential trend, it considers:  
          	•        	Does it fit the niche?  
          	•        	Does it fit the audience?  
          	•        	Does it fit the brand?  
          	•        	Can it naturally become useful content?  
   
If yes, it can incorporate it into future content.  
   
If not, it ignores it.  
   
23\. Comment Management  
   
The AI monitors comments on published content.  
   
Simple comments  
   
The AI can automatically respond.  
   
Example:  
   
          	“Thanks\!”  
   
Business questions  
   
The AI can respond using verified business information.  
   
Uncertain/important situations  
   
The AI sends the conversation to the owner.  
   
The AI must not invent:  
          	•        	Prices  
          	•        	Stock  
          	•        	Delivery times  
          	•        	Policies  
          	•        	Product information  
          	•        	Guarantees  
   
24\. Lead Detection  
   
The AI identifies potential buying intent.  
   
Example:  
   
          	“How much is this?”  
   
          	“I want two.”  
   
          	“How can I order?”  
   
The system identifies these as potential leads.  
   
The owner receives something like:  
   
          	🔥 Potential customer  
   
          	Customer is interested in the black sneakers.  
   
25\. Lead Conversion  
   
The AI can help move interested customers toward the business’s chosen sales channel.  
   
Possible destinations:  
          	•        	WhatsApp  
          	•        	Website/order page  
          	•        	Instagram DM  
          	•        	Facebook Messenger  
   
The business chooses its preferred channel.  
   
The AI uses that information when responding to customers.  
   
26\. Customer Follow-Up  
   
The AI can follow up with interested customers.  
   
Example:  
   
          	Customer: “I’ll order later.”  
   
The system can follow up after an appropriate period.  
   
Safeguards  
          	•        	No spam.  
          	•        	Reasonable follow-up frequency.  
          	•        	Business can disable automated follow-up.  
          	•        	Customer can end communication.  
          	•        	AI does not repeatedly contact uninterested people.  
          	•        	Sensitive/uncertain conversations are escalated to the owner.  
   
27\. Customer Memory  
   
The app should remember basic useful customer context, not everything.  
   
Example:  
   
          	Sarah  
Interested in: Black sneakers  
Asked about: Size 40  
Status: Didn’t purchase  
   
If Sarah returns later, the AI can use that context to make the conversation more useful.  
   
The system should avoid collecting unnecessary personal information.

31\. Product Differentiation  
   
The product should not be positioned as:  
   
          	“An AI that writes social-media captions.”  
   
That’s too easy to compare with generic AI tools.  
   
The stronger positioning is:  
   
          	An AI social-media employee for your business.  
   
It remembers your business, plans your content, creates the posts and visuals, adapts them to each platform, publishes them, watches the results, learns what works, handles simple customer interactions, identifies leads, and helps follow up.  
   
The business owner mainly needs to:  
   
          	Set the direction → Review → Approve.  
   
32\. Key Product Principle  
   
Every feature should pass this test:  
   
          	Does this reduce work for the business owner?  
   
If a feature requires the user to constantly make decisions, configure complicated settings, or manually manage content strategy, it should either be automated or hidden under optional advanced controls.  
   
33\. Product Success  
   
The product should ultimately make a business owner feel:  
   
          	“I don’t have to worry about what I’m posting every day anymore.”  
   
The strongest measure of success isn’t simply how many posts the AI generates.  
   
It is whether the app successfully moves the user from:  
   
“I need to post something.”  
   
to:  
   
“My social media is being handled.”
